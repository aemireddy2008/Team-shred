# Team-shreddi
Team-Shreddi

Design and Fabrication of Filament Shredder for Recycling FDM 3D Printing Waste

1. Project Overview

Team-Shredd is a mechanical recycling project designed to shred waste generated during Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM) 3D printing. The waste includes failed prints, support structures, and discarded plastic components. The system converts plastic waste into smaller pieces for potential recycling and reuse.

2. Problem Statement

FDM 3D printing produces plastic waste that is often discarded due to limited access to affordable recycling solutions. This contributes to plastic waste and material loss. Our project aims to provide a practical, low-cost mechanical shredding solution for laboratories, educational institutions, makerspaces, and small-scale manufacturers.

3. Proposed Solution

The proposed system uses rotating blades, stationary blades, spacers, a shaft, and a supporting frame to cut plastic waste into smaller pieces. The shredded material can be collected, sorted, and processed further for potential reuse in plastic recycling or filament production.

4. Key Features

* Mechanical shredding mechanism for 3D-printing waste.
* Rotating blade and spacer assembly.
* Stationary blades to support the cutting process.
* Supporting plates and frame for structural stability.
* CAD drawings for component design and fabrication.
* Potential for integration with a plastic filament recycling system.

5. Design and Components

The project includes CAD design files for components such as blades, spacers, and support structures. These files support the design, fabrication, and assembly of the shredder.

7. Working Methodology

1. Collect suitable FDM 3D-printing plastic waste.
2. Inspect and prepare the waste for shredding.
3. Feed the material into the shredding mechanism safely.
4. Rotate the cutting assembly to reduce the material into smaller pieces.
5. Collect and sort the shredded plastic for potential further recycling.

10. Future Scope

Future improvements may include motor-speed optimization, safety enhancements, improved cutting efficiency, and integration with a plastic extrusion system to produce recycled filament.

11. Safety

The shredder contains moving cutting components. Operation should use appropriate guards, emergency stopping arrangements, and supervision. Never place hands near the cutting assembly during operation.

12. Project Status

The repository provides CAD design and documentation files for the project. Prototype performance, shredding efficiency, and recycled filament quality will depend on fabrication, testing, and further development.
